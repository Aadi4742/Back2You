How Back2You Works

Four simple steps from losing an item to safely holding it back in your hands.

01

Report Misplaced Item

Provide item details, last known location on campus, description, and an optional reference photo.

02

Smart AI Matching

Our backend matching engine scores token keywords, categories, and campus zones to instantly suggest matches.

03

Submit Verification Claim

Provide unique proof of ownership (identifying marks, lock codes, or receipts) to prevent false claims.

04

Safely Returned

Collect your verified belonging at designated campus security desks or coordinate a safe handover.

Key Features

How the Platform Helps

Practical tools designed to help items find their way back to their rightful owners quickly.

Keyword & Location Matching

The backend compares words in the title, description, category, and building zone to highlight potential pairs automatically.

Proof of Ownership Check

Claimants describe hidden details like stickers, serial codes, or wallpapers so finders know they are returning it to the right person.

Notification Alerts

Get an in-app update whenever a potential match gets posted or someone files a claim on an item you reported.

Designated Collection Desks

Shows which campus desk is holding the item — whether it's at the Library 1st floor counter, gym desk, or main security post.

Admin Moderation Tools

Staff and security accounts can review submitted claims, update item status, and resolve handovers directly.

C++ and SQLite Backend

Built in standard C++17 with SQLite, keeping memory low and query responses fast without needing heavy external services.
